Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puli Meka - Tigers and Goats ("Puli Meka," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the Puli Meka mobile game.

1. Information You Provide

Puli Meka does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, or payment information directly to us in order to play.

The app uses third-party services for advertising, purchases, subscriptions, and app distribution. These services may receive or process certain information as described below.

2. Gameplay Data and Preferences

Puli Meka may store gameplay information and preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Game state or progress
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Difficulty selections
  • Sound preferences
  • Other settings needed for gameplay

This information is primarily stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own server for collecting your gameplay history or preferences.

Removing the app or clearing its local data may remove this information.

3. Advertising

Puli Meka uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements.

If you do not have an active Ad-Free subscription, advertisements may be displayed during gameplay or before certain actions, such as Undo or Restart.

Google Mobile Ads may receive or process information through its software development kit (SDK) for purposes such as displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, maintaining security, and providing advertising services.

Depending on your device, region, privacy choices, platform settings, and the services available to the app, this information may include:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, where available
  • IP address and general location derived from it
  • Advertisement interactions
  • App interaction information
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Where required by applicable law or platform requirements, the app may present privacy or advertising choices before using information for personalized advertising or other processing requiring consent.

We may configure advertising content restrictions and use AdMob controls to limit certain sensitive advertising categories.

For additional information about Google's privacy practices, please see Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Ad-Free Subscription and Purchases

Puli Meka offers an optional Ad-Free Yearly subscription.

While the subscription is active, advertisements normally displayed or required by the game are removed, including advertisements associated with supported actions such as Undo and Restart.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the marketplace from which you obtain the app, such as Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS.

We do not directly receive or store your complete credit-card, debit-card, or bank-account information.

The app may receive purchase or subscription status information from the applicable app store so that it can determine whether your Ad-Free access is active.

The subscription automatically renews according to the terms, billing period, and price presented by the applicable app store unless it is canceled. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your app-store account.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

We currently do not use a separate third-party analytics service specifically to analyze individual gameplay behavior.

Third-party services used by the app, including Google Mobile Ads, Google Play, and Apple platform services, may process diagnostic, performance, purchase, advertising, or app-usage information as necessary to provide their services.

Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies and applicable platform terms.

The app does not require users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other personal information directly to us in order to play.
